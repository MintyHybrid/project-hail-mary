The project

This compendium follows a single thread of reasoning: a catalytic-looking 15-nucleotide sequence that turned up in several potyvirus genomes, and what chasing it implies about horizontal sequence transfer and the host protein sorting machinery.

It is a preprint and work in progress, not peer-reviewed work. Several of its central ideas — DNAzyme activity, horizontal gene transfer, and the AP-4 / tyrosine-motif immune hypothesis — are stated as explicit, testable hypotheses rather than results, and are flagged as such throughout. Analyses, figures and conclusions may change.

Constructive feedback is welcome from anyone, regardless of background. The repository’s issue tracker is the best place for it.
